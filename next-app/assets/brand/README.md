# Google sign-in asset

`google-g.png` is Google's official multicolor G, sourced from
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png.
Used with Google sign-in text and as the Google company mark in named role previews; no recoloring.
Brand guidance: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.

# Lookout Buddy assets

Active original artwork: root assets/brand/lookout-buddy-master.png.
The active indigo/lavender Buddy has transparent outer pixels and pastel rainbow rays.
Website headers use the transparent 192x128 mark at 48x32, without a tile.
Browser favicon retains transparency. Installed app icons use a pale lavender
square background; the maskable variant has extra safe-area padding.

Archived approved style sheet: assets/brand/lookout-buddy-matcha-style-sheet.png.

## Landing motion study

`lookout-buddy-purple-idle.webp` is a transparent, full-body idle pose extracted with imagegen from the existing `assets/brand/lookout-buddy-style-sheet.png` motion study at the user’s request. It preserves hands, torso, legs and feet, with a palette-only edit to match the active purple/indigo and lavender character. The exact source and generation prompt are in `lookout-buddy-purple-idle.webp.json`. The raster stays still; CSS eye masks provide restrained glances and blinks. Three cyan, lavender and pink observation marks show during the centered viewer gaze and hide on side glances; paused/reduced-motion states retain the centered gaze and marks. Header/app icons keep their existing indigo identity.
