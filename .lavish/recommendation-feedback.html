Proposed behavior: keep each signal private and attach it to the exact recommendation and analysis version. Your agent can read aggregated feedback alongside your search preferences. A rating does not rewrite your profile, hide a job, approve outreach, or train a model.

Unrated stays unknown—not negative. Selecting the same answer again clears it. Optional reasons distinguish “wrong location” from “weak analysis,” so the agent does not learn the wrong lesson.
