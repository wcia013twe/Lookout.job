# interview-tracking

The Action Board is limited to Muse approvals and blockers. Generic assessments
(including HackerRank and HireVue) and scheduled interview descriptions stay out
of its action list; their dates remain visible in the weekly preparation
calendar beside the list. Networking follow-ups and AI Apply status are not
embedded there. The scoped website/MCP board projection and demo filter use the
same rule.

Validation: `npm run validate:data` passed. Database scenarios and hosted
deployment were not run.

Limits: Filtering uses saved action wording and does not infer events from email
text. This does not connect Lookout to an inbox or activate a background email
watcher. Muse email access depends on its separately authorized host connection.
