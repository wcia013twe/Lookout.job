# interview-tracking

The Action Board is limited to Muse approvals and blockers. Scheduled interview
confirmations and OA/assessment actions stay on the application record; prep
reminders and networking follow-ups stay in their owning workspaces. The scoped
website/MCP board projection and demo filter use the same rule.

Validation: `npm run validate:data` passed. Database scenarios and hosted
deployment were not run.

Limits: OA filtering relies on explicit task wording (OA, online/coding/technical
assessment, or take-home). This does not connect Lookout to an inbox or activate
a background email watcher. Muse email access depends on its separately
authorized host connection.
